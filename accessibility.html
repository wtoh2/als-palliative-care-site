About This Site

This site is intentionally designed to support eye-tracking systems and augmentative and assistive communication (AAC) technologies that act like a mouse or keyboard.

Site Navigation

  • All clickable elements are presented as large selectable buttons.
  • Focusing on a button highlights it, but activation only occurs when a click/selection command is sent.
    • A visible highlight / outline change indicates when a button is focused.
    • A yellow ring appears to confirm when a button has been activated.
  • Vertical scroll buttons ( ▲ ▼ ) are available on pages where resources are listed as an alternative to continuous scrolling.
  • Take your time. Nothing happens automatically or without your input.

Keyboard Navigation

If you use a keyboard or keyboard-based control:

  • Press Tab or Shift + Tab to move between buttons.
  • Press Enter to activate a button or link.

Privacy & Analytics

Your privacy matters. This site does NOT store personal information such as names, email, or IP addresses. Basic, anonymous usage data is collected in a manner fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA and PECR, and does not involve the use of cookies or similar tracking technologies. Specifically, this includes:

  • Number of website visits over time
  • Which filters and navigation paths are most used
  • How often each resource is clicked over time
  • Referring websites (e.g., did you come from INPCS or search engine)

This information is aggregated and may be used to understand how the site is being used and to improve usability.

Feedback & Accessibility Issues

If anything feels difficult to use, you notice any errors, or if you have suggestions for how we can improve this website, we would love to hear from you.
